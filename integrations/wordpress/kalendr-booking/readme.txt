=== Kalendr Booking ===
Requires at least: 6.0
Tested up to: 6.8
Requires PHP: 7.4
Stable tag: 1.0.0
License: GPLv2 or later

Show your Kalendr booking page on any WordPress page.

== Description ==
Add `[kalendr_booking]` to a page, or insert the "Kalendr booking" block. The plugin prints the same one-script embed that Kalendr's Share sheet gives you. It makes no request of its own and keeps no secret: it stores your Kalendr address and business name.

== Installation ==
1. Upload the kalendr-booking folder to /wp-content/plugins/, or upload the zip under Plugins, Add New.
2. Activate it.
3. Open Settings, Kalendr Booking and enter your Kalendr address and business name.
4. Add the shortcode or the block to a page.
