# One login, many businesses

A person who works at two businesses (a doctor at two clinics) uses one email, one password and one second factor, and switches between the businesses. Wave 2, built 25 Nov 2026. The consent step (25 Nov 2026) adds migration 0084 `staff_login_invites`.

## How it works

- **Active business.** An httpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookie `kalendr_business` holds the business's slug (`src/lib/active-business.ts`). `getStaffContext` picks, in order: a slug asked for by name (a caller or a URL; no match is a 403, never a fallback), then the cookie, but only if it names one of the person's **active** memberships, then the first business by name. A stale, forged or malformed cookie is ignored without a word. The cookie grants nothing; every request re-checks it.
- **Switching.** `POST /api/staff/business/switch` `{ slug }` checks the session and the membership only (not `getStaffContext`, so a business that wants a second factor blocks only itself) and sets the cookie. The sidebar header becomes a business menu (`business-switcher.tsx`, coss `Menu` with radio items: name, own role, a check on the open one) **only with two or more businesses**; otherwise the plain row stays. After a switch the client clears the query cache and the offline schedule (as sign-out does) and does a full page load of `/dashboard`.
- **No picker after sign-in.** The simpler option: with no cookie the person lands on their first business and the switcher is in the header. The screens that can leave someone stuck in one business (`/security/second-factor`, `/access-denied`) list the person's *other* businesses so a business that asks for a second factor, or that has not linked their profile, never locks them out of the rest.
- **Inviting an existing login: consent first.** `createStaffMember`, `createStaffPractitioner` (new doctor with a login) and `invitePractitionerLogin` call `findLoginToInvite` (`src/server/staff-login-invites.ts`). If the email already has a login (working elsewhere, invited elsewhere, or with no business at all) and is not on this team, nothing is granted: a row goes into `staff_login_invites` (migration 0084, forced RLS under the inviting business) with the role, job title, permissions, custom role, location limit and doctor profile the business chose. No membership, no access, no notifications, no calendar feed, and it does not count as "held elsewhere". Already invited or already on this team is still 409 `email_taken`.
- **The business can't tell the two apart.** The answer has the same keys as a new email's: an invite link (`/reset-password?token=…&invite=1`, a `staff-invite:` token that sets no password), a membership id (the id the membership will get) and a user id that is a random stand-in (`stand_in_user_id`), never the login's. The same `staff.member.created` / `staff.practitioner.created` audit row is written, and no "added to" notice is sent. The team list shows the invite as a row (`staff-repository.ts` adds invite rows to the member query) with the name and email the inviter typed, "Invite pending", the invite's expiry and no "Also works elsewhere" badge. Every team action works on the row and never touches the login: edit access, switch off, resend (a new invite link, never `shared_login`), cancel or remove (`loginDeleted: true`, as for a new person), link a profile, and change email (the invite becomes a new person's invite, whose login is made with the same stand-in id).
- **The person answers after signing in.** The email (`staff-invite.tsx`, `existingLogin`) says to sign in as usual and choose Accept or Decline; the link opens a card naming the business (`/reset-password`, `JoinInvite`). Signed in, a coss info `Alert` per invite (`pending-invites.tsx`) sits above every staff page and on `/access-denied`: "{business} invited you to join as {role}", with Decline and Accept. The list comes from `kalendr_staff_invites_for_user` (switched on, not declined, not expired, active business, never a platform operator) and shows the business name and a neutral role name only. `POST /api/staff/invites/[id]/accept` (session only, no business needed) makes the membership with everything the business chose, links the doctor profile if it has no login yet, deletes the invite, writes `staff.member.invite_accepted` in that business, sets the `kalendr_business` cookie and the page opens `/dashboard` there. `POST /api/staff/invites/[id]/decline` hides the invite from the person and kills its link; the business is not told (no audit row, the team row stays "Invite pending" until it expires or is cancelled), and a resend asks again. Someone else's invite, an answered, expired or switched-off one is 404.
- **What a business cannot do to a shared login.** Change their email (409 `shared_login`, `changeMemberEmail`) or send a password link (409 `shared_login`, `sendMemberAccessLink`; the link is shown to the sender, so it would let one business choose a password every business uses). It never sees their password, second factor or other businesses; the team list shows only a neutral "Also works elsewhere" badge (`worksElsewhere`, no business name). Removing or switching off a person in one business ends all their sessions (kept on purpose); they sign back in and land on another business, never the one that removed them.
- **Per-business rules still apply.** A business that requires a second factor refuses its own members at `getStaffContext`; the person opens another business from the switcher or the second-factor screen.

## Known limits

- The other businesses in the switcher show their name and the person's role there; the doctor/stylist role is called "Practitioner" for them, because that business's own words are private to it. No logo for them either, so their first letters stand in.
- A Web Push subscription is per business; switching does not move it, so alerts keep coming for the business it was turned on in. Turn them on again in the new business.
- Signing out leaves the `kalendr_business` cookie (a public slug, httpOnly); the next person to sign in on that browser ignores it unless they are in that business too.
- A person switched off in one business and still active in another is shown as working elsewhere only while the other membership is active.
- Audit rows older than this feature (`staff.member.created`, `staff.practitioner.created`) still hold the name and email; the new row does not.
- Consent leaks that remain: the inviter opening their own invite link sees "You're invited to join … sign in as usual" instead of "choose a password", and the two paths are not timed to match. Changing an invite's email to an address that already has a login still answers 409 (as before).
- Other people pickers (assigning work, mentions) list memberships only, so an accepted invite appears there only once accepted, as a new person does.
- An invite to a login that is later deleted, or to a platform operator account, stays "Invite pending" until it expires or is cancelled; the business is never told why.
- Accepting runs no business policy check (second factor) until the next page load in that business, where the usual check applies.
- The user id the business sees changes from the stand-in to the real login's when the person accepts (that happens only after consent).
