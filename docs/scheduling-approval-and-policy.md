# Approval rules, cancel and reschedule policy, bookings list (features 41, 42, 43)

Lane C (Scheduling), wave 2. Migrations `0045_approval_rules`, `0046_cancel_policy`, `0047_bookings_list` (renumbered after the trunk's 0042 to 0044; the snapshot chain continues from the trunk's 0044).

## Feature 41: approval rules

- **Rule per service.** `services.approval_rule` is `never`, `always`, `short_notice` (within `approval_notice_hours` of the start) or `free_mail` (the address is on a free-mail provider; the list is `src/server/data/free-mail-domains.json`, 50 domains). The one decision is `decideApproval` in `src/lib/approval.ts`, called by the booking commit. `services.booking_mode` is kept in step (`never` is `instant`, everything else `request`) for older code; the service editor's Approval tab is the place to change it. Migration 0045 turned every existing `request` service into `always`.
- **Approval is the only way a request becomes a booking.** `approveRequestIn` (`src/server/approval.ts`) is used by the staff `approve` action, by the older status endpoint (`requested → booked`) and by the bulk "confirm". A request on a service with `requests_hold_slot = false` holds no reservation; approving it re-checks the time in the same transaction and answers `409 request_slot_taken` when someone else has it ("choose another time").
- **Proposed times.** The booking body takes `proposedStarts` (up to 10); they are kept only when the booking waits for approval. Approving with `chosenStart` moves the request to one of them after the same free-time check.
- **Decline.** `decline` takes a fixed reason code (`fully_booked`, `outside_services`, `need_information`, `other`) and an optional message. The message travels in the outbox payload only; the audit row keeps the code and `hasMessage`. The manage page shows the reason label and a "Book another time" link.
- **Expiry.** Worker job `request-expire` (every minute) closes requests whose start has passed as `expired` (system actor, audit row, customer told). `request-reminder` (every 5 minutes) writes one staff inbox row when half of the time between asking and the start has gone (feature 56 will replace this with its own reminder).
- **Hide location.** `services.hide_location_until_confirmed`: while a request waits, the manage snapshot, the calendar file route, the confirmation and reminder emails and workflow messages leave out the address and call details.
- **Limits.** A waiting request on a non-holding service takes no capacity in booking limits; the per-customer cap still counts it.
- **Statuses.** `declined` and `expired` are new `appointment_status` values; both are terminal (`src/server/appointment-state.ts`).
- **Notices.** `appointment.status.booked` (approval, already mapped), `appointment.status.declined`, `appointment.status.expired`: built-in message (`src/worker/policy.ts`), email templates `request_declined` and `request_expired`, workflow trigger `cancelled`.

## Feature 42: cancel and reschedule policy

- **Pure rule.** `src/lib/cancel-policy.ts`: `customerCancelDecision`, `customerRescheduleDecision`, `cancellationDeadline`, `policyMessage`. The server (`src/server/booking-policy.ts`, inside `applyPatientAction`, so both the manage link and the portal) refuses with `403 cancel_not_allowed` or `reschedule_not_allowed` and a plain sentence with the business phone. The manage page only shows the same sentence instead of a button. Staff are never held to the customer cut-offs.
- **Seam for online payments (feature 23, lane D).** Read the free-cancellation deadline from `cancellationDeadline(policy, startsAt)` and the cancel reason from `appointments.cancel_reason_code` and `cancelled_by`. Do not keep a second cut-off. Cancellation, no-show and refund *money* rules are not built here.
- **Reasons.** Customer reasons are a fixed list (`customerCancelReasons`); notes (`cancel_reason_text`, `reschedule_reason_text`) are sealed with `sealPrivate` and opened only for staff screens; neither reaches audit metadata, outbox payloads or logs. Staff cancel with a business preset (`cancel_note_presets`; the label is copied to `appointments.cancel_preset_label`).
- **Ask the customer to move.** Staff action `request_reschedule` frees the slot and sets `reschedule_requested` (the booking keeps its id, token and history). The customer's message links to `/manage/<token>?reschedule=1`. Their answer books the new time (no policy cut-off applies to it) and the status returns to `booked`. Worker job `reschedule-expire` (every 5 minutes) cancels it as `system` with reason `reschedule_expired` after `services.reschedule_request_days` (default 7).
- **Same person.** `services.reschedule_same_person` on: a customer moving a booking can only keep the person. Off: the move may carry a `practitionerId` of anyone who offers the service here (the manage snapshot lists them).
- **Rebook.** Staff `rebook` makes a new booking for the same customer and service linked by `appointments.rebooked_from_id`; the old one is untouched.
- **Guests.** `edit_guests` (staff, any time before the visit; customer, until the reschedule cut-off) replaces the sealed guest list within the service's limit.

## Feature 43: bookings list

- **Query.** `src/server/bookings-list.ts`: one parameterised query built from a validated filter object (`src/lib/bookings-list.ts`), cursor paging on `(starts_at, id)`, tab counts only on the first page. Indexes `appointments_list_*` serve it. A practitioner sees only their own bookings.
- **Bulk.** `src/server/bulk-actions.ts`: up to 100 bookings; every booking runs the single-booking function in its own transaction with its own version check; the answer lists successes and failures with reasons.
- **Reassign / location / no-show.** `src/server/visit-actions.ts`. Reassign checks the person offers the service here and is free ("Dr Shah is busy at 10:30"), keeps the same booking and link, queues `appointment.reassigned` for the customer and writes an inbox row (`booking_moved`) for both people. A business no-show cancels the booking in the customer's favour and records `appointment_host_no_shows` for reports.
- **History.** `GET /api/staff/appointments/[id]/history` (`src/server/booking-history.ts`) reads the audit trail and sent messages; reasons are labels; sealed notes are never read.
- **Saved views.** `saved_views`, private or shared; only the owner removes one; the audit row names no filter values.

## Repeating tab (feature 43 and feature 17)

- **A screen-level tab.** `workspaceTabs` is `bookingTabs` plus `repeating` (`src/lib/bookings-list.ts`). The booking query, its counts and `tabCondition` keep working on the four booking tabs only (the booking route refuses `tab=repeating`); saved views accept the fifth tab and keep it in the stored filters like any other.
- **Query.** `GET /api/staff/bookings/series` (`src/server/series-list.ts`, filters validated by `seriesListQuerySchema`): one parameterised query, one row per series with the rule in words (`ruleSummary`, the business's own terms), the next visit still ahead (booked, requested, checked in), and visits done (completed or no-show) out of the visits still in the plan (cancelled, declined, expired and entered-in-error ones are not counted). Paged by cursor on (next visit, id); a series with nothing ahead sorts after the others; sort flips with the same `sort` word as the other tabs. The first page carries every tab's count plus `repeatingCount`, so the badge is right whichever tab is open; `GET /api/staff/bookings` carries `repeatingCount` too.
- **Filters.** Customer name or phone (`q`), person, service, branch, and `seriesStatuses` (`active`, `ended`, `cancelled`; nothing chosen means the running series). The other filters (booking status, booked via, dates, campaign) are kept in the address but do not apply to this tab, and the Filter menu hides them here. The Filter count counts only filters that apply.
- **Access.** Reading needs `appointments.status`, like the rest of the list, and a practitioner sees only their own series. Opening the series screen needs `appointments.book` (it is what `getSeriesView` checks), so without it the customer name is plain text and there is no open entry; Move and Cancel need `series.manage` and only appear for a running series with a visit still ahead.
- **Row actions.** The customer name and the row open `/appointments/series/[id]?from=bookings` (its back link returns to this tab). Move and Cancel open the same `SeriesScopeDialog` a single visit of a series uses, anchored on the next visit; "Just this one" goes to that visit's own screen. After a change the list reloads.
- **Isolation.** Every query runs inside `withTenant`; `appointment_series` has forced row-level security; `src/server/series-list.integration.test.ts` checks that another business's series are in neither the list nor the counts.

## Staff override and payments (added after the first report)

- **Staff override (feature 43, step 10).** Booking from the list (the shell's New appointment, which the list opens) into a time that is closed only by a soft rule (notice, how far ahead, limits) first answers `409 outside_booking_rules`; the screen then asks for one of the fixed reasons (`limitOverrideReasons`, the same list as limit overrides) and sends `limitOverrideReason`. `src/server/soft-rule-override.ts` skips only the soft rules: hours, time off, other bookings and rooms still apply, and a double booking of the same person is still refused. The reason code is in the audit metadata (`limitOverride`) with no free text, and History shows "Booked past the usual rules: ...".
- **Presets.** The first time anyone opens the list of cancellation reasons, a business with none (and no history of removing any) gets "<Practitioner> unwell" and "<Business> closed" in its own words (`seedDefaultPresets`).
- **Refund seam (features 23 and 42).** Every customer or staff cancellation, and every no-show, goes through `recordMutation`, which calls `applyPaymentPolicy` (`src/server/payments/cancellation.ts`): inside the free-cancellation window the deposit is queued for refund, outside it the fee stays as the stored policy says. `src/server/payments/business-fault-refund.ts` (`refundInFull`) refunds everything still refundable for a business no-show (`mark_no_show` with `who: "host"`) and for a booking the business asked to move that nobody rebooked (`reschedule-expire` job). Both only queue refunds for the worker; no provider is called in a request.
- **Speed.** The list test now seeds 100,000 bookings in one business and pages 10,000 of them, plus a deep page, each under 300 ms (typically a few milliseconds). The self-referencing `rebooked_from_id` foreign key needed `appointments_rebooked_from_idx`, or deleting appointments scans the table once per row.

## Known limits

- **Repeating tab (added with feature 17).** Built; see "Repeating tab" below. Its rows are series, so bulk actions and column choice do not apply to it.
- **Staff cancellations always follow the customer's payment policy** (feature 23), even when the reason is the business's own ("Doctor unwell"): a late staff cancellation can keep a fee unless staff refund more from the visit's payments screen. Only a business no-show and an unanswered request to move refund in full.
- **Workflow messages** for the new events: declined, expired, reassigned and location changes map to the existing `cancelled` and `rescheduled` triggers; there is no separate trigger.
- **WhatsApp and SMS** use the email path for the new notices until approved templates exist (`src/lib/channel-templates.ts` returns no template for them).
- **Same-person off** offers anyone who offers the service; smarter routing waits for feature 20.
