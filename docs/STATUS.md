### Product gaps (built around, not finished)

- **Messages:**
  - Many notices fall back to email because WhatsApp and SMS templates are missing: approvals, policy, waitlist, series, invoices, pay links.
  - Sending a pay link or an invoice by message is not built; staff copy the link instead.
- **Money:**
  - Class seats are not charged.
  - Packages and memberships can't be bought online.
  - A reschedule carries no price difference.
  - Message overage is counted but not billed.
  - No e-invoicing (IRN) and no GSTR export.
- **Staff app:**
  - No business switcher: someone in two businesses lands in the first one.
  - Hours settings shows both the old and the new schedule editor.
  - Test bookings have no badge.
  - The new calm motion is on the customer booking flow, the business page and the staff pages (a page entrance); the manage-booking page doesn't have it yet.
- **Teams and enterprise:**
  - SAML single logout and IdP-initiated sign-in are not supported.
  - The OIDC success path has not been tested.
  - Organisation defaults are stored but nothing applies them.
- **Discovery:**
  - Businesses on their own domain don't get a sitemap.
  - Services that accept only returning customers can now be booked by anyone, since booking no longer asks.
  - Reserve with Google is not built.
- **Tests:**
  - The manage-link browser test's accessibility check on the cancel dialog sometimes reports a colour-contrast finding and passes on a rerun; the cause is not found yet.
- **Apps:**
  - Zoho and Salesforce are not built.
  - The WordPress plugin has not been tried on a real site or listed.
  - The browser extension has no Firefox build.
- **Assistant:**
  - SMS chat is not connected.
  - Hindi safety phrases are only partly done.
  - Only English has fixed replies.
- **Operations:**
  - No metrics exporter: the rate-limit counter goes to logs only.
