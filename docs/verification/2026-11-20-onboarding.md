# Onboarding 2.0 (11): verification

Branch `claude/wave3b-brand-onboarding-25327b`. Migration `0056_onboarding_progress`.

What was run:

- `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm build`, plain `pnpm test`.
- Unit and component tests: the checklist rules (eight steps in order, automatic and manual steps, skips, finishing, half done), the go-live check, the day-1 and day-3 email rules (not sent to a finished or half-done owner; day 3 only without a real booking), all ten templates (valid hours, prices, words, a broad type with workflow presets), the owner-notice wording, the registration form (four short steps, no place or phone, the template and time zone sent, lands on the dashboard), the Getting started card (next step opens the right Settings section, all steps with skip and mark done, no hand tick for the test booking, go-live check in front of the link, hidden when finished or for an older business, sample data and removal after asking), the test booking panel (the business's own page filled in, polling, the schedule line, confirmation states, a phone number without a country code refused), and the tour (starts on the first visit to the dashboard of a business in the guided flow, resumes across pages, closes on Escape or the button, opens again from Help).
- Integration tests against a scratch PostgreSQL 17 on port 5601 with the web and worker roles without BYPASSRLS (`src/server/onboarding/onboarding.integration.test.ts`, 24 tests): registration fills services with prices, the owner's hours, a booking form and the business's words and is bookable at once on the public catalog; a business whose owner does not take bookings gets no person and no hours, the existing setup wizard's one gap is the person (it does not add the services again), and the go-live check says so; the four-detail registration; the eight steps and their order; leaving and returning resumes at the same next step; automatic steps are found done and the time is stored once; a step finished by doing it cannot be ticked by hand; the read-only support session may change nothing; no checklist for an older business; the tour states; the test booking found by the owner's email, flagged, the step done, the confirmation state read, one-click removal that leaves a real booking alone and unlinks (not deletes) the audit row, and that on a booking made on the public page with its form question answered still goes cleanly, retires a booking that holds a payment order instead of deleting money or failing, and keeps a customer who has a consent record (consent rows are append-only); every foreign key onto `appointments` and `patients` was checked; a booking before the panel opened or someone else's is not mistaken for the test; sample data labelled, once only, removed without touching real data, and no reminder, workflow message or email made for it after the reminder, workflow and message sweeps have each run twice (this caught the workflow planner making 14 scheduled messages for 8 sample bookings; fixed with `appointments.is_sample`); sample data refused without a service, person and hours; another business's test rows are untouched and the database function refuses to run out of scope; the go-live check flags and clears; the welcome email at once and a separate one per business; the day-1 email queued once and not at all to a finished or half-done owner, and stopped if the owner finishes before it is sent; the day-3 email only without a real booking (a test booking does not count); no email to an older business or one past two weeks; the admin funnel counts and median minutes behind the operator check.
- Browser journeys (`e2e/onboarding.spec.ts`, `e2e/owner-setup.spec.ts`, desktop and phone): a new owner registers in four short steps (axe clean), verifies the email and signs in, takes the five-stop tour, sees "Getting started 1 of 8", opens the test booking panel, books Haircut on their own page with the form already filled in (including the template's question), sees "It is on your schedule" and "Your confirmation is on its way as an email" (the worker sends to the capture server), sees the booking on the schedule, leaves and comes back at the same step, marks a step done, removes the test booking, gets the welcome email, and clears the go-live check by saving a phone, address and city through the page settings; sample data is labelled "(sample)" and removable; the go-live check names the missing phone and address and the leftover sample data. From the registration form to the test booking on the schedule took 13 to 20 seconds in the test, against a limit of 600.

Not verified: a confirmation on a real phone (needs a WhatsApp or text account; the worker was in capture mode), PostgreSQL 18 (17 was used), a real browser on a real phone.

Found on the way: `e2e/support/worker.ts` could not start the worker (`server-only` in the import chain since feature 14), so worker-based browser tests were not processing the outbox. Fixed with `e2e/support/tsconfig.worker.json`. And the provider-wide idempotency key for owner emails now includes the business id (see the hand-over note: the billing emails have the same problem).
