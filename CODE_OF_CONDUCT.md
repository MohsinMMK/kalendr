# Code of conduct

Kalendr follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about
the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing other
people's private information are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or
anywhere else the project is represented.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers by opening a private report
from the repository's **Security** tab, marked "Conduct". Reports are read only by the
maintainers and handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove content, and warn, suspend
or ban anyone who breaks this code.
